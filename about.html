About Us

Who We Are

Mayville Community Resource Centre is a community organisation created for this website project. The centre helps people access technology, training and useful information.

Our goal is to make technology easier to use and to create opportunities for learning and development.

Our Values

Community

We support people in our local community.

Respect

We treat everyone with respect.

Learning

We encourage people to learn new skills.

Support

We help people when they need assistance.
